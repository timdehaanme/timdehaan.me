What we do.

  1. 01

    Visionary deep-dive

    Two hours with you, including a DISC or Predictive Index profile.

  2. 02

    Accountability Chart

    Your org chart by responsibility: who owns what today vs. what the business needs.

  3. 03

    People Analyzer

    A confidential review of each leader against your core values and Gets it / Wants it / Capacity to do it.

  4. 04

    Leader conversations

    1:1s with 3 to 6 leaders, plus a short team survey.

  5. 05

    Readout working session

    Two to three hours together, ending with a 90-day plan.

What you walk away with.

  • A written assessment report
  • Your Accountability Chart: today and where it needs to go
  • A defined Integrator seat: its five core responsibilities, and whether it’s a full-time hire, an internal promotion or a fractional fit
  • Your top 3 people issues, named
  • A 90-day plan you can run with or without me

What it takes from you.

About 5 hours over 2 to 3 weeks, plus 45-minute conversations with your leaders.
