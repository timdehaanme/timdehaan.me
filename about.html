The 2-minute about me version

I started as a manufacturing engineer, working on packaging lines and boilers. From there I moved through maintenance, plant operations and operations management, and on to COO, President and CEO.

I’ve held the Integrator seat four times. At Harmonic Machine, I helped create the Visionary and Integrator roles when we launched EOS. At Advanced Material Handling, I ran all six EOS components. In 2024, I was named Global Integrator of the Year.

Now I do that work fractionally, for owners who need the seat filled but aren’t ready for a full-time hire.

Results from the seat

At Harmonic Machine, as COO, we grew to 100 people with 40%+ growth and record revenue, and were named the #6 Best Place to Work in Canada.

At Advanced Material Handling, as President and COO and then CEO, we cut turnover roughly in half, twice, and delivered the highest-output month in company history.

How I lead

Servant leadership. High standards and high support. My faith shapes how I treat people. Expect me to be calm, direct and honest.
